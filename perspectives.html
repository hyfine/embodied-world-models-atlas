Survey perspectives

Nine surveys, not nine mutually exclusive catalogs. Use their questions to cross-read the same 45 introductory works.

Pick a question and compare two works. These are editorial applications of the authors’ questions, not claims that the surveys cited the examples or endorsed the tags.

Duan · arXiv:2103.04918v8 · Fig. 6 · p.11

What does the platform, task or dataset supply?

Habitat supplies an interactive environment; R2R supplies routes, instructions and a task. Neither is the navigation controller.

Habitat ↔ Room-to-Room / VLN · Workbook direction and diagram →

Ma · arXiv:2405.14093v8 · Fig. 3 · p.3; Table III · p.8

Does the language interface emit a skill or a motor command?

SayCan selects a feasible skill; base OpenVLA emits quantized action coordinates. An instruction input does not determine the action head.

SayCan ↔ OpenVLA · Workbook direction and diagram →

Liu · arXiv:2407.06886v8 · Fig. 9 · p.11; §VII-B.1 · p.14

What separates recording interaction from executing a policy?

DROID supplies demonstrations; Diffusion Policy learns actions from demonstrations. Physical data collection and physical policy evaluation are different claims.

DROID ↔ Diffusion Policy · Workbook direction and diagram →

Ding · arXiv:2411.14499v4 · Fig. 2 · p.6; §5.5 · pp.28–29

Is generation used for interaction or to train another policy?

Genie lets the player steer generated frames. DIAMOND also uses generated Atari experience to train a policy. Similar outputs have different consumers.

Genie ↔ DIAMOND · Workbook direction and diagram →

Zhong · arXiv:2507.01925v1 · Table 6 · p.26; Table 8 · pp.33–34

What is the action format, and how many time steps does it encode?

Base OpenVLA uses tokens for one step; π0 emits a continuous chunk. These are not opposites: FAST can tokenize a whole action chunk (ledger main-163).

OpenVLA ↔ π0 · Workbook direction and diagram → · FAST

Li · arXiv:2510.16732v3 · Fig. 1 · p.3; Tables V–VIII · pp.15–16

Can two different representations serve the same decision role?

Both search over actions. PlaNet scores predicted reward; 2-AC scores future-to-goal feature distance. Shared use does not imply an identical objective.

PlaNet ↔ V-JEPA 2 / 2-AC · Workbook direction and diagram →

Wang · arXiv:2605.12090v1 · Fig. 3 · p.5; §4 · pp.14–26

Is an action derived from a predicted goal or generated with the future?

GE-Act 2.0 passes a visual plan to inverse dynamics. DreamZero generates visual futures with actions. Both fit J, but have different signal paths.

GE-Act 2.0 ↔ DreamZero · Workbook direction and diagram →

Zidan · arXiv:2606.00133v1 · §§3–6 · p.7; §§3.4.2, 4.1 · pp.27, 37

Is the imagined rollout consumed at training time or decision time?

PlaNet selects candidates at decision time; Dreamer trains an actor on imagined trajectories. Both learn dynamics but pay the computational cost differently.

PlaNet ↔ Dreamer · Workbook direction and diagram →

Yao · arXiv:2609.16697v1 · Table 2 · p.12; Table 4 · p.32

Does the test establish plausibility, intervention fidelity or decision utility?

DreamTrue tests action following in video; SIMPLER calibrates policy evaluation against physical trials. Neither test substitutes for the other or assigns a universal capability grade.

DreamTrue ↔ SIMPLER · Workbook direction and diagram →

How should I interpret these coordinates?

Profiles describe the illustrated execution or evaluation path, not every release. Control format may refer to an input to a predictor or an output of a policy; output span is not a planning horizon. OXE/RT-X is a compound record: E denotes the data and P the models; the dataset does not act.
