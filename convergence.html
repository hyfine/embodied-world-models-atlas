Where did WAM come from? Date the mechanisms, not a birthday

Reading convention

We use Wang et al.’s broad definition: a measurable future-state target coupled to action generation, whether cascaded or joint. Under Yao et al.’s evaluation lens, architecture says how a system works, not whether prediction improved a real outcome. Dates mark early public records; juxtaposition is not descent.

Wang et al. · Fig. 3, §§2–4 · Yao et al. · Figs. 2, 5; Table 4

1991 · Must a robot always model before acting?

Brooks builds behavior from sensor–action feedback; Dyna learns from real transitions and reuses simulated experience for decisions. These are alternative designs, not successive winners.

Intelligence without representation · Dyna

2016–2020 · How can a forecast change this decision?

Visual Foresight compares pixel motion under candidate pushes. PlaNet searches in latent state; Dreamer trains an actor on imagined trajectories instead of repeating the search at execution. What is predicted and when it is consumed are separate choices.

Deep Visual Foresight · PlaNet · Dreamer

2022–2024 · A parallel route: learn actions from language and demonstrations

RT-2 represents controls as tokens; Diffusion Policy generates continuous sequences. OpenVLA and π0 illustrate discrete and continuous vision-language-action interfaces. A policy can observe again after acting without generating a future video.

RT-2 · Diffusion Policy · OpenVLA · π0

2023 · The paths were already meeting in 2023

UniPi (first submitted 31 January) converts a text-guided video plan into controls using inverse dynamics; SuSIE connects refreshed subgoal images to a goal-conditioned policy. GR-1 (first submitted 20 December) predicts future images and actions from shared temporal context. These are early examples in the later WAM taxonomy, not a claim of coinage or priority.

UniPi · SuSIE · GR-1 · Wang et al. · Fig. 3, §§2–4

2025–2026 · From shared supervision to different execution paths

UWM changes the prediction task through separate noise levels for video and action. DreamZero explicitly called itself a WAM in February 2026; Fast-WAM retained video supervision without sampling future video at execution. Wang et al. published their synthesis of cascaded and joint approaches on 12 May. This dates a taxonomy, not the coinage of the term or the invention of its mechanisms.

Unified World Models · DreamZero · Fast-WAM · Wang et al. · Fig. 3, §§2–4

Make “unified” an inspectable interface

Let o be an observation, g a goal, a an action and f a possible future. A policy models p(a | o,g); a forward model predicts p(f | o,a). A WAM can model p(f,a | o,g): a cascade first proposes f and then produces a conditioned on o, g and f; a joint system learns coupled world and action targets. The probability factorization alone is not an implementation or a causal assertion: actions still change the physical environment. An external simulator used only for training or scoring does not turn the evaluated policy into a WAM.

Frontier watch · evidence through 2026-11-25

Cost at execution

Reported: Fast-WAM compares removing video co-training with skipping test-time video sampling; these ablations change different things. Atlas judgment: Expect more comparisons of training with foresight and computing it only when useful. Keep explicit sampling where it consistently improves recovery at matched hardware and deadlines.

Fast-WAM · DreamZero

Language–video–action alignment

Reported: In November 2026 SWAM studies language–video versus action supervision and compares mixtures under fixed training budgets. Its physical-task scope does not turn total hours into a universal scaling law. Atlas judgment: Hypothesis: alignment may explain transfer better than raw hours. It weakens if poorly aligned data reliably gives the same gains at matched budget and embodiment.

SWAM · Open X-Embodiment / RT-X

From scene changes to body response

Reported: The ζ0 preprint (22 Nov 2026) predicts visual features and joint trajectories and reports gains on two VLA bases. This is one non-pixel future, not coverage of arbitrary contact physics. Atlas judgment: Body or contact futures may matter where occlusion or slip determines success. Match observations, capacity and latency; if merely adding the sensor yields the same gain, credit cannot go to prediction.

ζ0 · The Feeling of Success · Yao et al. · Figs. 2, 5; Table 4

Terms: learn the job, not just the initials

WM · World Model · 世界模型

Predicts a subsequent state or decision-relevant quantity, possibly conditioned on an intervention.

Yao et al. · Figs. 2, 5; Table 4

VLA · Vision–Language–Action

Vision and language enter, actions leave. This interface alone does not tell us what physical knowledge is represented.

OpenVLA

WAM · World Action Model · 世界动作模型

Couples future-state modeling with action generation at system level; neither a single decoder nor video output is required.

Wang et al. · Fig. 3, §§2–4

MPC · Model Predictive Control · 模型预测控制

Compare candidate controls in a model, execute a prefix, then replan from a measurement.

PlaNet

IDM · Inverse Dynamics Model · 逆动力学

Infers a possible control from a state change. A pair of images need not uniquely determine the control.

UniPi

JEPA · Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture

Predicts representations rather than reconstructing pixels. Distinguish V-JEPA 2 pretraining from the 2-AC action-conditioned interface.

V-JEPA 2 / 2-AC

RL · Reinforcement Learning · 强化学习

Improves a policy using rewards or returns; experience may come from a real environment or model imagination.

Dreamer

Action chunk · 动作块

Several predicted controls per query. The predicted horizon need not equal the executed prefix.

Diffusion Policy

AR · Autoregressive · 自回归

A prediction conditions on preceding context; a shared temporal trunk need not imply a single vocabulary or output head.

GR-1

Diffusion · 扩散生成

Fit a denoiser and sample by reversing corruption. The target can be imagery or action; UWM separately controls the two noise levels.

Unified World Models

Ablation · 消融实验

Remove one component or objective under comparable conditions. Removing future supervision is not the same experiment as skipping runtime generation.
