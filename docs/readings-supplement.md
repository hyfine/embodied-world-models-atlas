# What does the robot actually use? / 机器人究竟用到了什么？

**Supplementary readings S1–S70 · 补充阅读 1–70** · [Row evidence / 逐行来源](../data/reading-supplement.json)

The same future image can be a training label, an imagined observation for RL, a plan to execute, or evidence for judging another policy. A useful first question is **who sees the future, when, and what would change if the prediction were wrong?** The comparisons below use the original authors' experiments, not a common leaderboard.

同一张“未来图像”，可能是训练标签、强化学习中的下一次观测、交给控制器的计划，也可能只是评价另一策略的依据。阅读这组工作时，先问**谁在什么阶段看到了预测，预测错了会改变哪个决策**，比按模型名称归类更有用。

**Scope / 范围。** S denotes the supplement row, not a unique model or publication. The 70 records cover **71 distinct papers and one first-party release article**: S51 contains three papers; S52 is the release. `sections` means selected body passages, figures, tables or proofs were inspected; for S52 it means named sections of the announcement. No record is `full`. Sections of code and model cards are additional evidence where identified, not proof of a successful run. Results below are author-reported. / S 是行号，不是论文计数。所列证据来自实际查阅的正文、图表、证明或发布文章栏目；没有把获取文件当成通读，也没有复现训练、实机试验或独立核验榜单。

## 1. A representation is not yet a planning cost / 能预测的表征，不一定能给动作排序

- **What does latent accuracy measure?** [C-SWM, S3 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.12247v2#S4.SS2) asks whether a predicted object configuration retrieves the right successor among alternatives. Its [evaluation code](https://github.com/tkipf/c-swm/blob/HEAD/eval.py#L114-L149) computes ranks, not rewards. [SOLD, S7 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08822v2#S4.SS2) instead tests whether an actor can *use* object slots: the advantage over an unstructured baseline is clearer when it must distinguish objects by their relations. [LPWM, S16 §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04553v1#S5.SS2) tests imitation separately from stochastic video generation.
- **What cost does the planner minimize?** [Value-guided JEPA, S13 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.00844v1#S4.SS2) evaluates goal-conditioned values with Wall/Maze start–goal pairs. [Temporal-Distance JEPA, S18 §§4.3–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25337v2#S4.SS3) holds the checkpoint, episodes and solver budget fixed and changes just the scoring function. A learned temporal distance helps navigation around barriers; on Push-T it can bring the agent into contact and still rank the subsequent pushes badly. Correlation with elapsed time is not a substitute for this intervention.
- **What information is retained until a decision?** [R2I, S4 §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.04253v1#S4.SS1) delays the relevant cue or reward to test state-space memory. [UniZero, S5 §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.10667v2#S3.SS3) keeps history accessible to a tree built from the current observation. [BMPC, S10 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18871v2#S4.F2) asks a different question: how much better is the *planner* than the network that proposes its actions? Reporting the search-assisted score alone would hide that gap.

中文读法：C-SWM 的命中率说明正确后继在候选中排得靠前；SOLD 的任务成功率才检验物体分槽是否帮助了操作。S13 和 S18 更进一步：即便表征能预测将来，规划仍需要一个能把好动作排在前面的代价。绕墙要考虑可达性，推块接触后还要精细调整姿态；两个目标不一定适合同一把尺。比较记忆或规划时，也应区分历史缓存、搜索预算与网络策略自身的贡献。

## 2. Who supplies the counterfactual? / 没有执行的动作，其“未来”从哪里来？

| Row | Model output and test / 输出与检验 | What does not follow / 不能据此推出 |
|---|---|---|
| [S21 DriveDreamer-2, §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.06845v2#S4.SS3) | A requested scenario becomes trajectories, road structure and multiview video. nuScenes comparisons separate no-image, video and multiview-first-frame conditions. / 从场景描述生成轨迹与多视角影像，按输入条件比较。 | Giving the model an initial view changes the task; a lower FVD is not a safer driver. / 不能忽略首帧条件，更不能把视频指标当作行车安全指标。 |
| [S23 OccSora, Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.20337v1#S2.F2) ↔ [S24 DrivingWorld, Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19505v3#S2.F2) | OccSora predicts semantic voxels through time; DrivingWorld predicts front-view tokens and ego pose. Compare [occupancy-token export](https://github.com/wzzheng/OccSora/blob/9f8d1f0da59c4163d2719f00093abc3f1dd3085a/step02.py#L223-L244) with a [scripted yaw intervention](https://github.com/YvanYin/DrivingWorld/blob/HEAD/tools/test_change_road_demo.py#L69-L82). / 一个输出时序占据，一个输出前视图像与自车位姿。 | Coarse geometry can miss moving-object detail; a plausible turn in a video need not make other agents react correctly. / 场景结构、图像逼真度、交通参与者的反应是三种不同检验。 |
| [S26 World Engine, §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19836v1#S4.SS2) → [S27 SimVTA, Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13781v1#S1.F2) | World Engine reconstructs logged scenes and models other agents. SimVTA renders selected candidate paths to give each a matched visual target during training. NAVSIM v1 PDMS goes from 86.2 to 91.3; HUGSIM's average closed-loop HD-Score is 38.8. / 前者提供仿真环境，后者用候选轨迹对应的渲染结果监督训练。 | These scores use different protocols and units. Off the logged path, a high video error can indicate a poor render rather than a poor predictor ([S27 App. G](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13781v1#A7)). / 偏离采集轨迹后，误差也可能来自渲染器。 |

The hardware boundary matters just as much. [Dream to Fly, S8 §V-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14377v2#S5.SS1) uses a real quadrotor **with rendered visual input**; it tests a dynamics gap, not the full camera-domain gap. [MAD, S20 Figs. 1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04534v1#S2.F2) instead learns occupancy and visibility from sensory input, but its deployed controller does not explicitly check the decoded map for safe paths. / 实机在环，不等于视觉也来自实机；能解码地图，也不等于部署时用地图做了可行性检查。

## 3. A future as teacher, plan, reward or judge / 未来作为教师、计划、奖励或裁判

| Role / 角色 | Diagnostic comparison / 可检验的区别 |
|---|---|
| **Teacher / 教师** | [FLARE, S29 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15659v1#S3.F2) aligns intermediate future tokens while training an action flow; [DreamVLA, S30 §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04447v3#S3.SS3) separately supervises motion regions, depth and semantics. [Being-H0.7, S49 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00078v1#S3.F2) aligns a current-context branch to one given future embeddings during training. None of these descriptions requires the deployed policy to see the true future. / 训练时监督中间表征，不等于测试时要拿到真实后续帧。 |
| **Input to control / 控制器的输入** | [mimic-video, S32 §III](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.15692v2#S3) compares an action decoder given predicted features with the same kind of decoder given an expert future. The latter is an oracle. [H-WM, S15 Fig. 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11291v3#S1.F1) instead predicts a logical transition and the visual endpoint of a subtask; the VLA executes at a faster rate. / 一个用专家未来做对照以定位误差，另一个预测子任务终点来指导执行。 |
| **Environment / 环境** | In [World4RL, S35 Fig. 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19080v2#S1.F1), imagined transitions and a separately trained success classifier refine an imitation policy. [WMPO, S63 §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09515v1#S3.SS2) explains why training the simulator only on expert successes can remove precisely the failures the policy must learn from. Its real insertion experiment first collects rollouts of the actual policy. / 想象训练不代表不需要真实交互，尤其是失败样本。 |
| **Reward / 奖励** | [SRPO, S36 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15605v2#S3.F2) compares *observed* rollouts in a video representation to shape progress. [NORA-1.5, S37 Fig. 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.14659v1#S3.F1) instead scores proposed actions by a *predicted* goal-latent distance or agreement with reference actions, then learns from preferences. / 用真实轨迹做相似度参照，与先预测动作后果再标偏好，不是同一种“世界模型奖励”。 |
| **Judge / 裁判** | [WorldEval, S43 §§3.2–4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19017v1#S3.F2) conditions video generation on Policy2Vec and compares a verifier's policy ranking with physical trials. Strong correlation across four policies does not calibrate every new policy's absolute success rate. / 能排出相对强弱，不等于能替代新策略的实机验收。 |

Two useful negative examples: [SANA-WM, S33 §5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15178v1#S5.SS3) follows **camera** trajectories; [WristWorld, S34 §4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07313v1#S4.SS4) produces an extra wrist **view** for a supplied anchor rollout. Neither experiment, by itself, tests closed-loop manipulation under a newly chosen action. / 相机走得对、腕部视角补得好，都是有用结果，但都不能单独证明新动作的物理后果预测正确。

## 4. Keep the denominator and the controller / 保留分母，也保留控制器

**S51 is three experiments, not one model release or one score.** / **S51 是三项独立研究，不宜合并成一个成功率。**

1. [Unified protocol, Figs. 1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22471v2#S2.F2): a task-specific knowledge package can be written from training examples and frozen for testing. Standard and extended interaction budgets are deliberately compared. / 冻结参数不等于没有利用训练集；延长交互预算也是条件变化。
2. [Paired RoboDojo study, §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38537v1#S3.SS2): **24/50** successes with a π0.5 hybrid versus **13/50** with direct control on the same evaluated instances. The 48% belongs to the pair of systems working together. / 48% 是混合控制，不是 Astra 单独执行，也不是整个榜单。
3. [GPT-GEAR, §6.2, Table 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28259v1#S6.SS2): **4/36** in the zero-shot LIBERO-GeoMotion test, where the variants emphasize geometric manipulation. Different tasks and a different action interface explain why this is not a contradiction of the previous number. / 此处测几何操作，任务、工具接口和分母都已改变。

The same rule applies elsewhere. [Wuji-hand post-training, S56 §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19666v1#S6) achieved 20/20 in each of five tasks, all using a marker; [FIND, S55 §IV-B](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32069v2#S4.SS2) reports 426 of 456 episodes without human scene recovery, **not** 426 completed tasks. [Motus, S31 §5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13030v2#S5.SS3) gives partial credit for physical subtasks. Even CALVIN columns need a split: [MoWM, S39 §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21797v3#S4.SS1) uses ABC → D, while [UD-VLA, S41 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.01718v2#S4.SS2) labels its comparison ABCD → D. / 完整任务成功、完成几个子步骤、不用人来复位，以及见过或没见过场景 D，各自回答不同问题。

## 5. What transfers, and what must be supplied again? / 迁移了什么，还要补什么？

**Data.** A usable human demonstration is more than an hour of video. [Open-AoE, S58 §5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14183v2#S5.SS4) counts uninterrupted history–future windows and valid hands; its annotation audit uses a 100-hour subset. [EgoVerse, S59 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07607v2#S3.F2) standardizes camera pose and hand keypoints across rigs. [S69 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21571v1#S3.F2) infers metric 3D hand motion and instructions from unscripted activity: coverage grows, but reconstruction errors become action-label errors. [TacClaw-Infinity, S57 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20320v2#S3.F2) adds a contact measurement to locate event boundaries and objects. Its reachability analysis and its policy-deployment table answer different questions.

**数据。** 连续性决定能切出多少训练窗口，位姿精度决定动作标签是否可信，触觉则能指出何时真正接触。由此不能把公开视频时长、可用双手轨迹时长和有力反馈的示范时长简单相加。物理接口同样不可省：[UMI-FT，S60 图3](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.09988v1#S4.F3) 把慢速策略与快速力控分开；[UMI-on-Air，S61 §IV-B](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.02614v3#S4.SS2) 用目标机体的跟踪代价引导采样，并在动捕提供机体状态的条件下做实机测试。Handheld data can transfer without target-robot demonstrations; this does not eliminate calibration, state estimation or embodiment-specific control.

**Adaptation.** Ask *where* the update acts. [Policy Decorator, S67 §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.13630v1#S4) learns a correction **after** the base policy produces an action. [DSRL, S65 Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15799v2#S4.F2) steers the noise **before** a fixed diffusion policy decodes it: some behaviors may remain unreachable. [PLD, S64 Fig. 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.00091v1#S3.F3) uses corrective specialists to *generate data* and deploys a retrained generalist. [DLR, S70 Fig. 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.19528v1#S1.F1) uses separate behavior modes to preserve diversity during RL data generation. These are not four interchangeable algorithms for finetuning backbone weights.

**适配。** 修正最终动作、改变去噪起点、让专家接管后把纠错轨迹教回通才、保留不同成功策略来扩充预训练数据，是四种不同路径。需要对比的不仅是成功率，还有在线试错发生在仿真还是真机、专家是否在推理时仍要运行、原策略本来就能覆盖哪些动作。
